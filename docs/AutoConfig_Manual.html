Overview

What does this do?

The AutoConfig is the brain behind your bot. It runs on a schedule and automatically decides which trading pairs to add, which ones to remove, and how aggressively to trade — so you don't have to do anything manually once it's running.

Think of it like a hiring manager that constantly scans the market, hires the best workers (active pairs), fires the lazy ones (stale pairs), and adjusts everyone's tasks based on current market conditions.

💡
Set and forget

Once configured, the bot manages everything. You only need to touch this file when market conditions change significantly (bull → bear, etc.).

Most Important Setting

The one line you need to change

There is one single setting that controls how the bot behaves across all market conditions. Everything else adapts automatically based on this:

autoconfig.json — HL_WM_AddTopVolume → filters → setMode
"setMode": {
  "type": "custom",
  "target": " (function(d){ global._wmMode='bear'; return true; })(this)"
}

Change the word inside the quotes to one of the three modes below:

📈
Bull Market
'bull'
Prices trending up. Bot hunts pairs with positive momentum. Most selective.
➡️
Sideways
'sideways'
Market going nowhere. Bot looks for volume surges. Balanced settings.
📉
Bear Market
'bear'
Prices dropping. Bot skips direction filters, trades both ways. Most relaxed.
⚠️
How to tell which mode to use

Look at Bitcoin's 24h chart. Clearly going up? → 'bull'. Mostly flat with spikes? → 'sideways'. Down significantly? → 'bear'. When unsure, use 'sideways'.

Capital Settings

Your money settings

These are the settings you'll want to personalise to your own account size. They live inside "overrides" in the HL_WM_AddTopVolume job.

Setting Default What it means Risk
CAPITAL 1000 Your total account balance in USD. Used for safety checks — not directly spent per trade. ● SAFE
TRADING_LIMIT 50 How much USD to put into each trade entry. With 10 pairs, this means up to $500 deployed at once. ● CAUTION
LEVERAGE 5 Multiplier on your position size. 5× means $50 controls $250 of exposure. Higher = more risk. ● HIGH RISK
maxPairs 10 Maximum number of pairs the bot will run at once. More pairs = more capital in play. ● CAUTION
MIN_NOTIONAL 11 Minimum trade size in USD. Hyperliquid requires at least $10 — keep this at 11 or above. ● SAFE
OF_COMPOUND true Automatically reinvests profits into the next trade. Recommended to leave on. ● SAFE
💰
Sizing rule of thumb

Set TRADING_LIMIT so that TRADING_LIMIT × maxPairs × LEVERAGE doesn't exceed 50–60% of your total balance. Example: $50 × 10 pairs × 5× leverage = $2,500 exposure on a $1,000 account is already aggressive.

Step by Step

How to switch market mode

1
Open autoconfig.json in any text editor Notepad, VS Code, or any editor works. Find the file in your Gunbot folder.
2
Search for the word setMode Use Ctrl+F (Windows) or Cmd+F (Mac) to jump right to it.
3
Change the market mode word You'll see 'bear', 'sideways', or 'bull' in that line. Replace just that word (keep the single quotes).
4
Save the file Ctrl+S or Cmd+S. Gunbot will pick up the change on its next scheduled run — no restart needed.
🚫
Only change the mode word — nothing else on that line

The line looks complex. You only need to change the one word between single quotes: 'bear', 'sideways', or 'bull'. Don't remove the quotes, spaces, or any surrounding characters.

Rules

Do's and Don'ts

✅ SAFE TO CHANGE
  • Market mode ('bull' / 'sideways' / 'bear')
  • CAPITAL — your account size
  • TRADING_LIMIT — per-trade size
  • LEVERAGE — multiplier
  • maxPairs — max active pairs
  • exclude — pairs to never trade
🚫 DO NOT TOUCH
  • Any schedule values (timing)
  • Any type values
  • Filter names or filter logic
  • FUTURES_EXCHANGE or WM_PRESET
  • OF_COMPOUND settings
  • The strategy name
The 5 Jobs

What each job does

The AutoConfig has 5 automated jobs. They all run on their own schedules — you don't start or stop them manually.

📥 HL_WM_AddTopVolume

Scans Hyperliquid every 5 minutes and adds high-volume, volatile pairs that pass the market-mode filters. This is where your market mode setting lives.

⚙️ HL_WM_SetOverrides

Runs every 5 minutes on active pairs. Checks each pair's current spread and volatility, and gates or unblocks trading entries accordingly. Adapts to your market mode automatically.

🗑️ HL_WM_RemoveDropped

Removes pairs that have fallen out of the top volume rankings and have no open position. Runs every 10 minutes. Keeps your pair list clean and focused.

💤 HL_WM_RemoveStale

Removes pairs that have been sitting idle for 4+ hours with no open position and a dead market. Frees up slots for better opportunities.

🖥️ HL_WM_StartGUI

Checks if any active pairs exist and automatically starts the Gunbot GUI if they do. You'll normally never notice this job — it just works in the background.

Common Questions

FAQ

No pairs are being added — what's wrong?
First, check your market mode. If the market is bearish and you're in 'bull' mode, the filters will block almost everything. Switch to 'bear'. Also, the bot needs at least 1 hour of snapshot data (12 snapshots × 5 min) before it can evaluate pairs properly after a first start.
A pair was added but not trading — why?
The SetOverrides job may have detected a spread that's too wide or not enough volatility (ATR) on that specific pair right now. This is intentional — the bot waits for better conditions before entering rather than forcing a bad trade.
The bot keeps removing pairs I want to keep
If a pair drops out of the top volume rankings and has no open position, it gets removed. This is by design — the strategy focuses on the most liquid pairs only. If you want a specific pair excluded from removal, add it to the exclude field in the RemoveDropped job.
BTC is excluded — can I add it?
BTC (USD-BTC) is excluded by default because its price is very high and it behaves differently to altcoins for this strategy. You can remove it from the exclude field, but it's not recommended unless you have experience with BTC perpetuals.
Do I need to restart Gunbot after changing the mode?
No. Gunbot reads autoconfig.json on each scheduled run. Save the file and the next time the AddTopVolume job fires (within 5 minutes), it will use the new mode.
Can I run this on multiple exchanges at the same time?
This AutoConfig is configured specifically for Hyperliquid. Do not change the exchange field to another exchange without first consulting your bot administrator, as the pair format and settings differ per exchange.
